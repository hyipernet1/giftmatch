What exactly do I get for $0.99?
+
A list of 10–15 personalised gift matches based on your quiz answers, plus access to send your first e-card. After 48 hours, your account converts to GiftMatch Plus ($29.99/month) for unlimited e-cards and unlimited quiz retakes — we'll email you 24 hours before any charge.
How do I cancel my subscription?
+
One click in your account settings. No phone calls, no "are you sure?" loops, no retention offers. We're required by EU law to make it as easy to cancel as it was to sign up — and we genuinely think that's how it should be.
Where do gifts ship from?
+
All our partner brands ship within the EU. Most orders arrive in 2–5 business days with VAT pre-calculated for your country. We don't ship the gifts ourselves — we link you directly to each brand's checkout.
Is my data safe?
+
Yes. We're fully GDPR-compliant. We don't sell your data, we don't track you across the web, and we don't even require an account to take the quiz. Read our privacy policy for the boring-but-important details.
What if I don't like the matches?
+
Email us within 14 days and we'll refund your $0.99, no questions asked. EU consumer rights also entitle you to cancel any digital purchase within 14 days unless you've used the service — we honor that fully.
